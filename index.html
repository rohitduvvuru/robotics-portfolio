01

MULTI-ROBOT AUTONOMY · REINFORCEMENT LEARNING

Dynamic Multi-Robot Formation Control

A ROS 2 autonomy and learning framework for robot teams that reconfigure when robots join, leave, or are externally disturbed while continuing toward a shared destination.

ROS 2PPOGNNPyTorchGymnasiumGazebo

Problem

Keep coordinated navigation robust when the active team composition changes at runtime.

My work

Built the Gymnasium-compatible multi-robot environment, trained and evaluated the GNN-based PPO policy, and developed the surrounding ROS 2 autonomy framework for coordinated navigation, reconfiguration, and autonomous joining/leaving.

Learning design

Used per-robot control, agent masking, teleoperation disturbances, collision and goal rewards, and formation-error metrics. A single policy was used across changing formation sizes.

RESULTEpoch 1000

My trained policy consistently reached the goal while maintaining formation during navigation.

ENGINEERING LESSON

Robot turning constraints exposed a sim-to-real mismatch, reinforcing the importance of physics-aware training and hardware validation.

02

REINFORCEMENT LEARNING · MOBILE ROBOTICS

Autonomous Wall Following with Q-Learning & SARSA

LiDAR-based wall following formulated as a discrete MDP and trained with tabular reinforcement learning in Gazebo.

ROSGazeboQ-LearningSARSALiDAR

Method

Three LiDAR sectors, discretized distance states, motion primitives, epsilon-greedy exploration, reward shaping, and expert-initialized Q-tables.

Evaluation

Compared Q-learning and SARSA across 300 episodes and tested the greedy policy on straight walls, turns, and an unseen isolated wall segment.

Outcome

SARSA produced the stronger and smoother late-training reward trend while the learned policy transferred to the disconnected I-shaped geometry.
